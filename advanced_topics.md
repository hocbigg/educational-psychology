## Advanced Topics in Educational Psychology

**Track Index:**

- **Learning Sciences & Instructional Design** — The applied science of designing effective learning experiences, drawing on cognitive science, instructional design theory, and educational technology.
- **Assessment & Psychometrics** — Advanced measurement theory and assessment methodology, including classical test theory, item response theory, and knowledge inference.
- **Special Education & Inclusive Practices** — Designing learning environments that support diverse learners, including disability inclusion, universal design, and specific learning difficulties.
- **Motivation, Self-Regulation & Evidence-Based Practice** — Advanced motivational science, self-regulated learning theory, and evidence synthesis for identifying what actually works in education.
- **Educational Neuroscience** — The neural mechanisms underlying learning, including neurobiology, brain-development research, and critical evaluation of neuromyths.

## Learning Sciences & Instructional Design

The applied discipline of designing, implementing, and evaluating effective learning environments, drawing on cognitive science, instructional design theory, and educational technology.

Instructional Design Foundations: [Instructional Design Foundations and Applications (University of Illinois, Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/instructional-design-foundations-applications) - a systematic introduction to instructional systems design, covering analysis, design models, and the development of instructional solutions to performance problems.

Learning Sciences as a Field: [The Cambridge Handbook of the Learning Sciences, 3rd ed. (R. Keith Sawyer, ed.)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781108744669) - the definitive reference work for the interdisciplinary field of learning sciences, with 33 chapters synthesizing research on core topics including how to design educational software, prepare teachers, and organize productive learning environments.

Learning Technologies: [Ubiquitous Learning and Instructional Technologies (University of Illinois, Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ubiquitouslearning) - covers currently available technologies for learning including learning management systems, intelligent tutors, computer adaptive testing, gamification, simulations, and learning analytics.

Educational Data Mining & Learning Analytics: [Big Data and Education MOOT (Ryan S. Baker, University of Pennsylvania)](https://learninganalytics.upenn.edu/MOOT/bigdataeducation.html) - a complete open video textbook covering prediction modeling, knowledge inference, behavior detection, relationship mining, and structure discovery applied to educational data.

## Assessment & Psychometrics

The science of measuring educational and psychological constructs, covering validity theory, test construction, classical test theory, item response theory, and the practical use of assessment data.

Classroom Assessment & Measurement: [Classroom Assessment and Educational Measurement (Brookhart & McMillan, eds.)](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/oa-edit/10.4324/9780429507533/classroom-assessment-educational-measurement-susan-brookhart-james-mcmillan) - an open-access edited volume connecting classroom assessment practice with educational measurement theory; chapter 4 on feedback and chapter 8 on embedded assessment are particularly valuable for advanced learners.

Classical Test Theory vs. Item Response Theory: [ITEMS Portal (National Council on Measurement in Education)](https://ncme.elevate.commpartners.com) - a free, openly accessible portal containing instructional modules on measurement theory, including a nontechnical comparison of CTT and IRT frameworks written by leading psychometricians.

Knowledge Inference & Structure Discovery: [Knowledge Inference and Structure Discovery for Education (PennX, edX)](https://www.edx.org/learn/data-analysis/university-of-pennsylvania-knowledge-inference-and-structure-discovery-for-education) - covers advanced methods for inferring student knowledge from data and discovering the underlying structure of knowledge domains.

## Special Education & Inclusive Practices

The design of learning environments that effectively support learners with disabilities and diverse needs, covering inclusive systems, universal design, and specific learning difficulties.

Systems of Support for Diverse Learners: [Disability Inclusion in Education: Building Systems of Support (University of Cape Town, Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/disability-inclusion-education) - explores the support teachers need to meet the needs of children with severe to profound hearing, visual, and intellectual disabilities, with input from teachers, activists, and experts in inclusive education.

Universal Design for Learning: [UDL Guidelines 3.0 (CAST)](https://udlguidelines.cast.org) - the definitive framework for designing learning environments that reduce barriers and honor learner variability, now updated to address biases and systems of exclusion; essential for any practitioner designing inclusive instruction.

Dyslexia & Literacy Difficulties: Overcoming Dyslexia (Yale University, Coursera) - search 'Overcoming Dyslexia' on Coursera; taught by Sally Shaywitz, one of the world's leading dyslexia researchers, this course covers the science of reading difficulties and evidence-based interventions.

## Motivation, Self-Regulation & Evidence-Based Practice

The advanced study of what drives learning and how to optimize it, covering self-regulation theory, evidence synthesis methodology, and the cognitive science of effective learning strategies.

Self-Regulated Learning Theory: [Becoming a Self-Regulated Learner: An Overview (Zimmerman, 2002)](https://www.leiderschapsdomeinen.nl/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/Zimmerman-B.-2002-Becoming-Self-Regulated-Learner.pdf) - the seminal paper defining self-regulated learning as a self-directive process rather than a fixed ability, outlining the three phases (forethought, performance, self-reflection) and their underlying motivational beliefs.

Evidence Synthesis & Effect Sizes: [Visible Learning: A Synthesis of Over 800 Meta-Analyses Relating to Achievement (Hattie, 2008)](https://inspirasifoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/John-Hattie-Visible-Learning_-A-synthesis-of-over-800-meta-analyses-relating-to-achievement-2008.pdf) - the foundational evidence synthesis comparing the effect sizes of hundreds of influences on student achievement, establishing the 0.4 hinge point as the benchmark for a year's growth.

Desirable Difficulties: [Making Things Hard on Yourself, But in a Good Way: Creating Desirable Difficulties to Enhance Learning (Bjork & Bjork, 2011)](https://bjorklab.psych.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2016/04/EBjork_RBjork_2011.pdf) - explains how conditions that make learning harder in the short term (spacing, interleaving, contextual variation) can dramatically improve long-term retention and transfer.

Evidence-Based Interventions Ranked: [Hattie's Effect Size List: 256 Influences Related to Achievement (Visible Learning)](https://visible-learning.org/hattie-ranking-influences-effect-sizes-learning-achievement) - a continuously updated ranking of influences on student achievement with effect sizes, allowing practitioners to identify which interventions have the strongest evidence base.

## Educational Neuroscience

The interdisciplinary field connecting neuroscience research to educational practice, covering the neural basis of learning, brain development, and critical evaluation of brain-based claims in education.

Neurobiology of Learning: [Understanding the Brain: The Neurobiology of Everyday Life (University of Chicago, Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/neurobiology) - a comprehensive introduction to functional human neuroanatomy and neuronal communication, then applies this foundation to understanding perception, voluntary movement, and social behavior.

Advanced Neuroscience for Education: [Medical Neuroscience (Duke University, Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/medical-neuroscience) - a rigorous, graduate-level survey of the neural systems that mediate sensation, action, and cognition, providing the deep neuroanatomical foundation needed to critically evaluate educational neuroscience claims.

Neuromyths & Critical Evaluation: [Neuromyths in Education: Prevalence and Predictors of Misconceptions Among Teachers (Dekker et al., 2012)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3475349) - the landmark study documenting that teachers on average believe 49% of common neuromyths, particularly those related to commercialized educational programs; essential reading for anyone wanting to distinguish genuine neuroscience findings from marketing claims.
