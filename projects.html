Design your own lesson plans

  • Understanding by Design Teaching Guide (Vanderbilt University Center for Teaching) - A step-by-step walkthrough of backward design covering desired outcomes, acceptable evidence, and aligned learning experiences.
  • Cognitive Load Theory in Practice: Examples for the Classroom (NSW Department of Education) - A practitioner manual detailing how to structure lesson plans, worked examples, and cognitive scaffolds to prevent working-memory overload.
  • Understanding by Design Framework (Grant Wiggins & Jay McTighe, ASCD) - The foundational white paper detailing the three stages of unit design, the six facets of understanding, and the core UbD planning template.

Construct your own scoring rubrics

  • Know Your Terms: Holistic, Analytic, and Single-Point Rubrics (Cult of Pedagogy) - A comparative breakdown of the three primary rubric formats with concrete criteria for choosing among them.
  • Meet the Single Point Rubric (Cult of Pedagogy) - A step-by-step tutorial on building streamlined rubrics that isolate performance standards while allowing tailored qualitative feedback.
  • Using Rubrics for Assessment and Grading (Center for Teaching Innovation, Cornell University) - A university guide on defining objective performance dimensions, drafting descriptive level indicators, and calibrating scoring reliability.
  • VALUE Rubrics (American Association of Colleges and Universities) - Open-access, nationally validated rubric templates spanning critical thinking, quantitative literacy, and inquiry-based learning.

Formulate your own behavior intervention plans

  • Functional Behavioral Assessment: Identifying the Reasons for Student Behavior (IRIS Center, Vanderbilt University) - An interactive training module guiding learners through antecedent-behavior-consequence (ABC) data collection, hypothesis formulation, and functional analysis.
  • What Is a Behavior Intervention Plan? (Child Mind Institute) - A clinical guide breaking down the non-negotiable components of an effective Behavior Intervention Plan (BIP), including prevention strategies, replacement behaviors, and reinforcement schedules.

Develop your own measurement scales

  • Developing Questionnaires for Educational Research: AMEE Guide No. 87 (Anthony R. Artino Jr. et al.) - A comprehensive, seven-step methodological guide to operationalizing constructs, pretesting items with cognitive interviews, and evaluating scale validity.
  • Repository of Measurement and Survey Design Resources (Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab) - An extensive library of methodological tools, questionnaire modules, and survey design protocols for measuring educational outcomes.
  • FS995: A Step-By-Step Guide to Developing Effective Questionnaires and Survey Procedures (Rutgers NJAES) - A structured circular detailing question format selection, response-bias mitigation, and sampling procedures for educational program evaluation.

Construct your own logic models

  • Logic Model Development Guide (W.K. Kellogg Foundation) - The standard manual covering program theory of change, input-activity matrices, output mapping, and short- and long-term outcome indicators.
  • Enhancing Program Performance with Logic Models (University of Wisconsin–Madison Division of Extension) - An interactive course with downloadable templates and worked educational examples for articulating an intervention's underlying rationale.

Design your own program evaluations

  • CDC Program Evaluation Framework Action Guide (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention) - An 84-page manual for structuring evaluation questions, gathering credible evidence, and establishing performance standards across community and educational programs.
  • W.K. Kellogg Foundation Evaluation Handbook (James Sanders) - A practical guide to developing project-level evaluation blueprints, managing primary data collection, and synthesizing findings for stakeholder action.
  • Conducting Mixed-Methods Research: From Classical Social Sciences to the Age of Big Data and Analytics (Venkatesh, Brown, & Sullivan, Virginia Tech Publishing) - An open-access methodological textbook detailing research design, data integration, and meta-inferences in mixed-methods evaluations.
  • The Rainbow Framework (BetterEvaluation) - A comprehensive task-based guide organizing the end-to-end evaluation lifecycle into seven clear methodological clusters.

Run your own improvement cycles

  • Basics of PDSA Cycles (Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching) - An introduction to improvement science methodology and rapid-iteration Plan-Do-Study-Act testing in educational settings.
  • Plan-Do-Study-Act (PDSA) Form (Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching) - A practical template for articulating predictions, defining data collection procedures, and documenting change-idea iterations.
  • Plan-Do-Study-Act (PDSA) Worksheet (Institute for Healthcare Improvement) - The standard worksheet format for tracking small tests of change, analyzing observed outcomes against predictions, and planning subsequent implementation steps.

Write your own policy briefs

  • Policy Briefs (UNC Writing Center) - A structural guide contrasting policy writing with academic prose, focusing on audience-tailored recommendations and executive summaries.
  • How to Write a Policy Brief (International Development Research Centre) - Practical instructions on synthesizing research findings into persuasive, action-oriented policy recommendations.
  • Policy Briefs 101: A Guide for Translating Research Findings into Policy Impact (California Policy Lab) - A clear guide featuring structural templates, design tips, and an actionable checklist for translating research into policy deliverables.
