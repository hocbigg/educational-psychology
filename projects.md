## Build a Program Evaluation

- [CDC Program Evaluation Framework Action Guide](https://stacks.cdc.gov) - practical, step-by-step guidance for planning and conducting a program evaluation, from identifying stakeholders to reporting findings
- [W.K. Kellogg Foundation Evaluation Handbook](https://www.betterevaluation.org/sites/default/files/2022-07/EvaluationHandbook.pdf) - a widely used handbook for planning and implementing project-level evaluation in education and community programs
- [Conducting Mixed-Methods Research](https://publishing.vt.edu) - a 276-page open-access book covering principles, strategies, and guidance for combining qualitative and quantitative evaluation methods
- [Monitoring and Evaluation Guide](https://www.ifrc.org) - a six-step framework for planning and managing monitoring and evaluation systems, applicable to educational programs

## Build a Logic Model

- [W.K. Kellogg Foundation Logic Model Development Guide](https://www.naccho.org/uploads/downloadable-resources/Programs/Public-Health-Infrastructure/KelloggLogicModelGuide_161122_162808.pdf) - the standard 72-page guide to developing program logic models, covering inputs, activities, outputs, outcomes, and impact
- [Logic Model Templates (University of Wisconsin Extension)](https://logicmodel.extension.wisc.edu) - free downloadable templates plus an interactive course on logic model development and use in program design
- [Basic Logic Model Template (Wisconsin DPI)](https://wlresources.dpi.wi.gov) - a one-page starter template for sketching out a program's theory of change

## Write a Policy Brief

- [Policy Briefs (UNC Writing Center)](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/policy-briefs/) - a clear handout on how policy briefs differ from other academic writing, with guidance on audience, tone, and structure
- [How to Write a Policy Brief (IDRC)](https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/funding/resources-idrc-grantees/how-write-policy-brief) - a focused guide emphasizing purpose-driven structure, audience targeting, and linking evidence to actionable recommendations
- [Policy Briefs 101: A Guide for Translating Research Findings into Policy Impact (California Policy Lab)](https://www.capolicylab.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Policy-Brief-Guide.pdf) - a 6-page PDF with a side-by-side comparison of academic writing versus policy-brief writing, plus a practical checklist

## Run an Improvement Cycle (PDSA)

- [Basics of PDSA Cycles (Carnegie Foundation)](https://www.carnegiefoundation.org/basics-of-pdsa-cycles) - a Summit session introducing the Plan-Do-Study-Act improvement cycle with sample templates and tools
- [Improvement Science Collection (Carnegie Foundation)](https://www.carnegiefoundation.org/improvement-science-collection) - a curated library of free resources including PDSA worksheets, driver diagram guidance, and case-based simulations
- [PDSA Worksheet (Institute for Healthcare Improvement)](https://www.ihi.org) - a downloadable template with instructions and a worked example for documenting Plan-Do-Study-Act cycles

## Design and Run a Survey

- [A Step-By-Step Guide to Developing Effective Questionnaires (Rutgers NJAES)](https://njaes.rutgers.edu) - a practical guide to questionnaire construction and survey procedures for program evaluation
- [Survey Design Resources (J-PAL)](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/resource/repository-measurement-and-survey-design-resources) - a curated repository of measurement and survey design tools, including K-12 education-specific measurement challenges
- [Evaluation Method: Surveys How-to-Guide (University of Wisconsin Extension)](https://health.extension.wisc.edu) - an 8-step process for designing surveys for needs assessment or program evaluation

## Build a Professional Portfolio / Deliverable

- [How to Build a Standout Professional Portfolio in Learning Design (Digital Learning Institute)](https://www.digitallearninginstitute.com) - a step-by-step guide for showcasing instructional design and educational psychology work
- [How to Write Instructional Design Case Studies for Your Portfolio (The eLearning Coach)](https://theelearningcoach.com) - guidance on adding case studies that demonstrate methodology and results, supplementing a skills-based portfolio
- [Write a Better Instructable! (Instructables)](https://www.instructables.com) - a meta-guide on how to structure a step-by-step tutorial or how-to guide, useful for producing educational artifacts and instructional walkthroughs
