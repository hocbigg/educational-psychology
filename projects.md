## Design your own lesson plans

- [Understanding by Design Teaching Guide (Vanderbilt University Center for Teaching)](https://cft.vanderbilt.edu/guides-sub-pages/understanding-by-design/) - A step-by-step walkthrough of backward design covering desired outcomes, acceptable evidence, and aligned learning experiences.
- [Cognitive Load Theory in Practice: Examples for the Classroom (NSW Department of Education)](https://education.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/main-education/about-us/educational-data/cese/2017-cognitive-load-theory-practice-guide.pdf) - A practitioner manual detailing how to structure lesson plans, worked examples, and cognitive scaffolds to prevent working-memory overload.
- [Understanding by Design Framework (Grant Wiggins & Jay McTighe, ASCD)](https://www.ascd.org/ASCD/pdf/siteASCD/publications/UbD_WhitePaper0312.pdf) - The foundational white paper detailing the three stages of unit design, the six facets of understanding, and the core UbD planning template.

## Construct your own scoring rubrics

- [Know Your Terms: Holistic, Analytic, and Single-Point Rubrics (Cult of Pedagogy)](https://www.cultofpedagogy.com/holistic-analytic-single-point-rubrics/) - A comparative breakdown of the three primary rubric formats with concrete criteria for choosing among them.
- [Meet the Single Point Rubric (Cult of Pedagogy)](https://www.cultofpedagogy.com/single-point-rubric/) - A step-by-step tutorial on building streamlined rubrics that isolate performance standards while allowing tailored qualitative feedback.
- [Using Rubrics for Assessment and Grading (Center for Teaching Innovation, Cornell University)](https://teaching.cornell.edu/teaching-resources/assessment-evaluation/using-rubrics) - A university guide on defining objective performance dimensions, drafting descriptive level indicators, and calibrating scoring reliability.
- [VALUE Rubrics (American Association of Colleges and Universities)](https://www.aacu.org/initiatives/value-initiative/value-rubrics) - Open-access, nationally validated rubric templates spanning critical thinking, quantitative literacy, and inquiry-based learning.

## Formulate your own behavior intervention plans

- [Functional Behavioral Assessment: Identifying the Reasons for Student Behavior (IRIS Center, Vanderbilt University)](https://iris.peabody.vanderbilt.edu/module/fba/) - An interactive training module guiding learners through antecedent-behavior-consequence (ABC) data collection, hypothesis formulation, and functional analysis.
- [What Is a Behavior Intervention Plan? (Child Mind Institute)](https://childmind.org/article/what-is-a-behavior-intervention-plan/) - A clinical guide breaking down the non-negotiable components of an effective Behavior Intervention Plan (BIP), including prevention strategies, replacement behaviors, and reinforcement schedules.

## Develop your own measurement scales

- [Developing Questionnaires for Educational Research: AMEE Guide No. 87 (Anthony R. Artino Jr. et al.)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4059192/) - A comprehensive, seven-step methodological guide to operationalizing constructs, pretesting items with cognitive interviews, and evaluating scale validity.
- [Repository of Measurement and Survey Design Resources (Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab)](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/resource/repository-measurement-and-survey-design-resources) - An extensive library of methodological tools, questionnaire modules, and survey design protocols for measuring educational outcomes.
- [FS995: A Step-By-Step Guide to Developing Effective Questionnaires and Survey Procedures (Rutgers NJAES)](https://njaes.rutgers.edu/fs995/) - A structured circular detailing question format selection, response-bias mitigation, and sampling procedures for educational program evaluation.

## Construct your own logic models

- [Logic Model Development Guide (W.K. Kellogg Foundation)](https://wkkf.issuelab.org/resources/10124/10124.pdf) - The standard manual covering program theory of change, input-activity matrices, output mapping, and short- and long-term outcome indicators.
- [Enhancing Program Performance with Logic Models (University of Wisconsin–Madison Division of Extension)](https://logicmodel.extension.wisc.edu/) - An interactive course with downloadable templates and worked educational examples for articulating an intervention's underlying rationale.

## Design your own program evaluations

- [CDC Program Evaluation Framework Action Guide (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention)](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/256296/cdc_256296_DS1.pdf) - An 84-page manual for structuring evaluation questions, gathering credible evidence, and establishing performance standards across community and educational programs.
- [W.K. Kellogg Foundation Evaluation Handbook (James Sanders)](https://wkkf.issuelab.org/resources/9191/9191.pdf) - A practical guide to developing project-level evaluation blueprints, managing primary data collection, and synthesizing findings for stakeholder action.
- [Conducting Mixed-Methods Research: From Classical Social Sciences to the Age of Big Data and Analytics (Venkatesh, Brown, & Sullivan, Virginia Tech Publishing)](https://doi.org/10.21061/conducting-mixed-methods-research) - An open-access methodological textbook detailing research design, data integration, and meta-inferences in mixed-methods evaluations.
- [The Rainbow Framework (BetterEvaluation)](https://www.betterevaluation.org/rainbow-framework) - A comprehensive task-based guide organizing the end-to-end evaluation lifecycle into seven clear methodological clusters.

## Run your own improvement cycles

- [Basics of PDSA Cycles (Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching)](https://www.carnegiefoundation.org/basics-of-pdsa-cycles) - An introduction to improvement science methodology and rapid-iteration Plan-Do-Study-Act testing in educational settings.
- [Plan-Do-Study-Act (PDSA) Form (Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching)](https://www.carnegiefoundation.org/improvement-science-collection) - A practical template for articulating predictions, defining data collection procedures, and documenting change-idea iterations.
- [Plan-Do-Study-Act (PDSA) Worksheet (Institute for Healthcare Improvement)](https://www.ihi.org/resources/tools/plan-do-study-act-pdsa-worksheet) - The standard worksheet format for tracking small tests of change, analyzing observed outcomes against predictions, and planning subsequent implementation steps.

## Write your own policy briefs

- [Policy Briefs (UNC Writing Center)](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/policy-briefs/) - A structural guide contrasting policy writing with academic prose, focusing on audience-tailored recommendations and executive summaries.
- [How to Write a Policy Brief (International Development Research Centre)](https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/funding/resources-idrc-grantees/how-write-policy-brief) - Practical instructions on synthesizing research findings into persuasive, action-oriented policy recommendations.
- [Policy Briefs 101: A Guide for Translating Research Findings into Policy Impact (California Policy Lab)](https://www.capolicylab.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Policy-Brief-Guide.pdf) - A clear guide featuring structural templates, design tips, and an actionable checklist for translating research into policy deliverables.
